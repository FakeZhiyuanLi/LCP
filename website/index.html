A class is more than a transcript.

One place for
everything that matters.

Record on Monday. Find the passage you need when it’s time to study. Bring the right context into every conversation.

Keep the room

Pick a class, press Record, and save the audio to its lecture history. Recordings stay on your Mac unless you set up a synced destination.

Find the thread

Search class readings and saved transcripts. A citation takes you back to the exact passage you kept, even after a source is revised.

Share with intent

Choose the materials to share. Export a readable file for chat, or grant a compatible MCP client access to selected class sources.
